# Support My Work

Ways you can support my work and help keep independent creation and open-source development alive. If you've benefited from my themes, tools, or writing and want to help keep it going, here are several ways to provide support.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-coins"></i> The Toonie Club

The Toonie Club is my approach to creative support: **one simple tier, full access for everyone.** Named after the Canadian two-dollar coin, it's **$2 CAD per month** (or pay whatever you want)—no expensive gated content, no confusing tier benefits, no FOMO. Just simple, sustainable support for my writing and free coding projects. Read more about it at [brennan.day/toonie-club](https://brennan.day/toonie-club/).

## <i class="fa-solid fa-sack-dollar"></i> Financial Support

- <i class="fa-solid fa-mug-saucer"></i> **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/brennan)**: Join the Toonie Club or leave a one-time tip (0-5% fees, instant payouts) — **recommended**
- <i class="fa-brands fa-github"></i> **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/brennanbrown)**: Support my open-source development directly (0% fees, monthly payouts)
- <i class="fa-brands fa-patreon"></i> **[Patreon](https://patreon.com/brennankbrown)**: Toonie Club membership, $2 CAD/month—same single tier as everywhere else

## <i class="fa-solid fa-hand-holding-heart"></i> Non-Monetary Support (Free & Invaluable!)

Not everyone can contribute financially, and that's perfectly okay. Your time and engagement are just as valuable as financial support. Here are meaningful ways to help that don't cost money:

### 1. Share My Work
- Share my articles and essays on social media (Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn)
- Recommend my [themes and tools](/projects) to friends, colleagues, or your dev community
- Link to my projects from your blog, portfolio, or documentation
- Write about your experience using my tools

### 2. Star or Contribute
For the technically-inclined:
- <i class="fa-solid fa-star"></i> Star one of [my repositories](https://github.com/brennanbrown) on GitHub
- <i class="fa-solid fa-bug"></i> Open issues for bugs or feature requests
- <i class="fa-solid fa-code-branch"></i> Submit pull requests to improve my projects
- <i class="fa-solid fa-pen-to-square"></i> Help with documentation by correcting typos or adding examples
- <i class="fa-solid fa-flask"></i> Fork and experiment with my themes and tools

### 3. Refer Clients
Help my web development business, [Berry House](https://berryhouse.ca):
- Introduce me to nonprofits, collectives, or indie creators
- Recommend my services to organizations needing JAMstack development or content strategy
- Connect me with projects aligned with my values
- [Book a consultation](https://calendly.com/brennanbrown/consult) if it's you who needs the work done

### 4. Join the Community
- Join [Write Club](https://writeclub.ca) if you're in Calgary
- Subscribe via RSS to [brennan.day](https://brennan.day/feed.xml)
- Follow me on social platforms (Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn)

### 5. Collaborate
- Invite me to guest post on your blog
- Ask me to appear on your podcast
- Propose joint projects or collaborations
- I'd love to give shout-outs to cool projects you're working on

### 6. Speaking Opportunities
I'm available for talks on:
- The IndieWeb and decentralized web technologies
- Indigenous issues in tech
- The craft of writing
- Independent creator economics and open-source development

*(Virtual talks preferred, but open to Calgary-based in-person events)*

### 7. Feedback & Engagement
- <i class="fa-solid fa-comment"></i> Comment and engage with my blog posts
- <i class="fa-solid fa-envelope"></i> [Email me](mailto:mail@brennanbrown.ca) with thoughts or criticism
- <i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i> Suggest ideas for future content or tutorial topics
- <i class="fa-solid fa-star-half-stroke"></i> Leave reviews on Amazon for my published books

### 8. Stay Connected
- <i class="fa-solid fa-rss"></i> Subscribe via RSS to [brennan.day](https://brennan.day/feed.xml)
- <i class="fa-solid fa-share-nodes"></i> Follow me on social platforms

## Why Support Matters

When you support independent creators, you're:
- Building an internet that doesn't rely on surveillance capitalism
- Valuing quality over quarterly earnings
- Helping small creators compete with big tech platforms
- Supporting free and open-source software that developers worldwide rely on
- Participating in an economy of care and connection

Every share, star, comment, or contribution helps create a sustainable alternative to the enshittifying platforms that dominate our digital lives.
